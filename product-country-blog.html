Recommendation: YES

Part 2 Recommendation

Yes, based on this section, I would still recommend the product for Vietnam. Vietnam's trade links with Canada and its mixed economy make entry possible, while the culture and etiquette research show that success depends on respect, patience, and relationship building. The CPTPP and other Canada-Vietnam links make the market easier to enter than a country with weaker trade ties. At the same time, this section shows that Farm Boy would need to adapt its approach and not assume Canadian business habits will work the same way in Vietnam. This means the market is workable, but only if Farm Boy uses the right local business approach.

Political and economic fit

Vietnam allows business activity and foreign trade, which supports product entry.

Trade support

CPTPP and other agreements make exporting smoother than it would be without trade support.

Culture

Respect, formality, and relationship building matter a lot, so Farm Boy would need to adapt.
