Overall Recommendation: YES

Final answer

Overall, I recommend that Farm Boy sell the Eda-Yummy Spicy Kale Edamame Dip in Vietnam, but only as a small premium launch at first. The product has a good chance of success because Vietnam has a large urban population, strong trade links with Canada, and a growing market for imported foods in major cities. It also helps that the product is different from many common local dip options, which gives Farm Boy a way to stand out if it is marketed well.

Farm Boy should not try a wide national launch right away because the product is chilled, expensive, and aimed at a niche customer group. It would likely do best with middle and upper income shoppers who already buy imported food in premium stores. This means the launch should stay focused on the strongest markets instead of trying to reach every customer in Vietnam.

The best plan is to launch in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City through premium supermarkets and a local cold-chain distributor. This gives the product a better chance because those cities have the most realistic customer base for a premium imported chilled dip. If the product performs well there, Farm Boy could later expand to more stores or more cities. If sales are weak, Farm Boy can stop early without taking on the risk of a much larger launch.

Main reasons for the final decision

  • Vietnam is big enough to support a niche imported product.
  • CPTPP and strong trade ties make entry more realistic.
  • The product is different from common local options.
  • Farm Boy already has a strong Canadian base.
  • A simple launch plan already exists for target market, price, promotion, and distribution.
