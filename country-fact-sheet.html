Country Fact Sheet

This section looks at the size of the market, where people live, and the basic economic picture in Vietnam.

Recommendation: YES

Part 1 Recommendation

Yes, based on the country facts, I would recommend trying to sell this product in Vietnam. Vietnam has a very large population, major urban shopping centres, and strong trade connections, which all support market potential. The country also has major cities like Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City where imported products are more likely to find the right customers. The low GDP per capita means the product should be sold as a niche premium item, not a mass market product. This part supports a small and careful launch instead of a wide launch across the whole country.

Why this part matters

  • It shows Vietnam is big enough to support a niche imported product.
  • It shows where the strongest starting markets would be.
  • It shows that price will matter because average income is much lower than in Canada.
